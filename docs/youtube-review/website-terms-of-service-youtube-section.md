### YouTube Platform Integration Terms

#### 1. YouTube API Services

Star Gaze integrates with YouTube through YouTube API Services. Subject to availability, your subscription or account permissions, and your authorization, the integration may allow you to connect a YouTube channel, view channel information, upload and schedule videos, select video metadata and visibility, view publishing history, synchronize comments, request comment translations, and submit replies to comments.

Your use of YouTube features within Star Gaze is also subject to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms), the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), and applicable YouTube and Google policies. By accessing or using YouTube API Services through Star Gaze, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service in addition to these Star Gaze Terms of Service.

#### 2. Authorization and account connection

You must connect a YouTube account through Google’s authorization process before using features that require access to private channel data or actions on your behalf. You represent that you are authorized to control the connected Google account and YouTube channel and to grant the requested permissions.

Star Gaze requests only the OAuth permissions currently needed for visible YouTube features. The `youtube.force-ssl` permission is used because YouTube does not provide a narrower authorization scope that supports user-initiated comment replies. That permission may technically allow broad actions involving videos, ratings, comments, and captions; Star Gaze uses it only for the features described in our user interface, these Terms, and our Privacy Policy.

You must not connect an account or channel that you do not own or have lawful authority to manage. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your Star Gaze and Google accounts and for activity performed through your authorized account.

#### 3. User direction and final control

Star Gaze performs actions on YouTube only in response to your instructions or settings. Before an action is submitted, you are responsible for reviewing the selected YouTube channel, content, title, description, tags, category, audience designation, synthetic-media disclosure, visibility, publication time, and comment reply text.

Where a visibility option is available, you retain final control over whether a video is submitted as private, unlisted, or public. Star Gaze will not intentionally change the visibility of existing YouTube content without your instruction.

Comment replies are sent only after you enter, select, or confirm the reply. You are responsible for ensuring that replies are accurate, lawful, appropriate, and compliant with YouTube policies. Translation output may contain errors and must be reviewed by you before it is used in a reply.

#### 4. Content responsibility

You retain your rights in content you submit through Star Gaze. You grant Star Gaze the limited rights reasonably necessary to process, transmit, and submit that content to YouTube at your direction and to provide the related user-facing service.

You are solely responsible for content and metadata that you upload, publish, schedule, or submit through Star Gaze, including videos, music, images, titles, descriptions, tags, comments, replies, commercial disclosures, and synthetic or altered media disclosures. You must have all rights, licenses, permissions, and consents necessary for the content and its intended use.

You must comply with applicable laws, the YouTube Terms of Service, YouTube Community Guidelines, copyright and publicity rights, advertising and consumer-protection rules, and any disclosure obligations that apply to artificial, synthetic, altered, sponsored, or commercial content.

You must not use Star Gaze to upload or submit unlawful, infringing, deceptive, abusive, harassing, malicious, or unauthorized content, to impersonate another person, to manipulate engagement, to send spam, or to circumvent YouTube safeguards or access controls.

#### 5. Publishing, comments, and platform decisions

YouTube independently controls processing, moderation, availability, visibility, monetization, removal, and other treatment of content on its services. Star Gaze does not guarantee that a video or reply will be accepted, processed, displayed, made public, recommended, monetized, or retained by YouTube.

Uploads made by an API project that has not completed a required YouTube API Services compliance audit may be restricted by YouTube, including being limited to private visibility. Star Gaze is not responsible for restrictions imposed by YouTube, Google, an API quota, an account limitation, a policy review, or a third-party service.

YouTube data shown in Star Gaze may be delayed, incomplete, or temporarily unavailable because of API behavior, quota limits, authorization status, network conditions, moderation, or platform changes. You should verify critical information directly in YouTube.

#### 6. Privacy and data use

Our collection, use, storage, refreshing, sharing, and deletion of YouTube and Google user data is described in the [Star Gaze Privacy Policy](https://toryxai.com/privacy). That policy includes the categories of data accessed, purposes of use, retention periods, translation processing, security controls, revocation methods, and data-deletion procedures.

You acknowledge that Star Gaze may use service providers to operate infrastructure, storage, security, monitoring, support, and user-requested translation features, subject to the safeguards described in our Privacy Policy.

#### 7. Disconnecting and revoking access

You may stop Star Gaze’s future access to your YouTube account by disconnecting the YouTube account through Star Gaze or by revoking Star Gaze’s authorization from [Google Account third-party connections](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).

Disconnecting or revoking access may prevent pending uploads, schedules, comment synchronization, translations, or replies from completing. You should cancel or review pending tasks before disconnecting.

Disconnecting Star Gaze or deleting data stored by Star Gaze does not delete content or other data stored by YouTube. You must use YouTube or another authorized application that supports the relevant deletion action to delete YouTube-hosted data.

You may request deletion of YouTube-related data stored by Star Gaze as explained in the Privacy Policy.

#### 8. Suspension and termination

We may limit, suspend, or terminate access to YouTube-related features where reasonably necessary to address a security incident, invalid or revoked authorization, suspected fraud or abuse, legal requirement, API quota or platform restriction, or an actual or suspected violation of these Terms, the YouTube Terms of Service, or applicable Google or YouTube policies.

Where reasonably practicable, we will provide notice or an opportunity to correct the issue. Immediate action may be taken where necessary to protect users, Star Gaze, Google, YouTube, or the public.

#### 9. Changes to the integration

YouTube or Google may change, restrict, suspend, or discontinue API features, scopes, quotas, policies, or services. We may update or discontinue the YouTube integration to remain secure and compliant or to respond to platform changes. Material changes to our processing of Google or YouTube user data will be reflected in our Privacy Policy and, where required, presented for renewed consent.
