Privacy, Data Subject Rights and Retention Policy

Organization: Wuhan Guanxing Cultural Media Co., Ltd. (Brand: Star Gaze)
Version: 1.1
Effective Date: April 13, 2026

Statement. Star Gaze is a service brand operated by Wuhan Guanxing Cultural Media Co., Ltd. This document describes how Star Gaze handles privacy requests, data retention, deletion, and contract-end disposal for platform and TikTok-related data.

1. Scope

This policy applies to personal data and platform-related data processed by Star Gaze in connection with account registration, TikTok authorization, TikTok Shop integration, content publishing, and customer support.

2. Categories of Data Processed

  • Account data, including email address, phone number, username, and account profile information.
  • TikTok-authorized data, including basic profile identifiers, OAuth tokens, and linked account information required to provide publishing functions.
  • TikTok Shop showcase information used to help users select products for shoppable content creation.
  • User-uploaded or generated media files processed for content publishing.
  • Operational logs and security logs necessary to maintain service integrity and investigate incidents.

3. Data Subject Rights

Star Gaze supports reasonable requests to access, correct, update, delete, or revoke authorization for personal data, subject to identity verification and applicable legal requirements.

  • Users may submit privacy or deletion requests through the published contact channel: toryxai@outlook.com.
  • Star Gaze verifies the requester identity before acting on a request that involves personal data or account changes.
  • Valid requests are reviewed and processed within a reasonable period, generally within 30 days.
  • Users may revoke TikTok authorization at any time through TikTok account settings. Star Gaze will then delete associated TikTok data within 30 days, unless retention is required by law.

4. Data Retention and Disposal

Data Category Purpose Retention Rule Disposal Method
Account profile data Provide account access and platform functions Retained while the account is active and for a limited period after closure where required by law or dispute handling needs Deleted or anonymized after account closure when no longer required
TikTok OAuth tokens and linked account identifiers Support authorized publishing and account connection Retained until expiration, revocation, or re-authorization need Deleted when authorization is revoked or no longer needed, generally within 30 days
TikTok Shop showcase data Allow product selection for shoppable content Fetched in real time where possible and not permanently stored beyond operational need Cleared from local processing/storage when no longer needed
Uploaded video/media files Prepare and publish content Temporary processing only Deleted from service storage after successful upload or when the task is cancelled and no longer needed
Support and security logs Support operation, troubleshooting, and security review Retained for a limited operational period consistent with legitimate business and compliance needs Deleted or rotated according to internal retention schedules

5. Contract Termination and Merchant Data Disposal

When the business relationship or service contract ends, Star Gaze will stop processing customer data except where continued retention is required by applicable law, dispute resolution obligations, or documented security investigation needs. Customer and TikTok-related data under Star Gaze control will be deleted or anonymized within a reasonable period, generally within 30 days.

6. Storage Regions

Based on the current service architecture and published privacy disclosures, Star Gaze stores and processes relevant data on infrastructure located in the United States. Data handling for the US market is restricted to US-based infrastructure.

7. Privacy Policy Maintenance

Star Gaze maintains a published Privacy Policy and reviews it periodically when legal requirements, product features, or data processing practices change. Material updates are reflected on the official website.

8. Privacy Contact

Privacy-related requests and questions may be submitted to: toryxai@outlook.com.

9. Review and Update

This policy is reviewed periodically and updated when required by operational, legal, or platform compliance needs.
